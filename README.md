## 📍 About the Project 📍
Eid Card is a lightweight web page that turns a simple greeting into a joyful experience. Colorful balloons float across the screen, confetti keeps bursting, and a 3D greeting card opens to reveal your personal message. Type the recipient's name, your name, and your wishes, and the card updates instantly.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Floating Balloons** 🎈: Nine animated balloons spell out "HAPPY EID!"
- **Confetti Celebration** 🎉: Confetti bursts automatically every 3 seconds
- **Interactive 3D Card** 💌: Hover over the card and the cover swings open to reveal the message inside
- **Animated Greeting** ✨: The "عيدكم مبارك" text rotates, floats, and fades on the card cover
- **Personalized Message** ✍️: Edit the recipient name, sender name, and message live
- **Mobile Friendly** 📱: Built to be opened and shared from your phone

---

## 💻 Tech Stack

- 🧱 **HTML5**: Page structure and the card form
- 🎨 **CSS3**: Balloon animations, 3D card flip, gradient background, keyframes
- ⚡ **JavaScript**: Live card updates and confetti scheduling
- 🎊 **js-confetti**: Confetti animation library (loaded from CDN)
- 🌐 **GitHub Pages**: Hosting the live demo
- 🐙 **GitHub**: Version control

---

## 📱 How It Works

1. **Open the Page (👋)**
   - Balloons float at the top and confetti starts bursting.

2. **Open the Card (💌)**
   - Hover over (or tap) the card to flip the cover and see the message inside.

3. **Personalize It (✍️)**
   - Fill in **Recipient's Name**, **Sender's Name**, and **Your message here**.

4. **Update the Card (🔄)**
   - Press **Update Card** and your text appears on the inside of the card.
